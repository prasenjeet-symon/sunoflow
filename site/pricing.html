Billing questions

Straight answers.

What happens when the trial ends?
Your subscription starts at ₹99 a month and carries on until you cancel. If you'd rather not continue, cancel any time during the seven days and you won't be charged.
Can I cancel whenever I want?
Yes. There's no minimum term and no cancellation fee — it's a monthly subscription, so you're never committed beyond the month you're in.
Is there more than one plan?
No, and that's deliberate. There's one plan with everything in it, so there's no feature you'll discover you're locked out of halfway through writing something.
Is there a limit on how much I can dictate?
No. Talk as much as you like — there's no word count, no daily cap and no meter running in the background.
Do I get new features as they arrive?
Yes. Improvements come as part of the subscription. There's no paid upgrade to a bigger version later on.
What do I need to run it?
A Mac with Apple silicon (any M-series machine) running macOS 13 or later. The first time you open it, SunoFlow asks for permission to use your microphone and to type into other apps.
