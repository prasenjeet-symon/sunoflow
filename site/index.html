The cost of typing

The keyboard is the slowest
part of your day.

You already know what you want to say. Typing is just the toll you pay to get it out of your head — and you pay it every single time you open a reply box.

Most people speak around three times faster than they type. SunoFlow hands you that difference back.

Typing~40 words a minute
Talking~150 words a minute

How it works

One shortcut.
That's the whole thing.

Nothing to open, nothing to switch to, nothing to copy and paste afterwards.

1

Press the shortcut

Click wherever you were about to type and press ⌥ Space. A small bar appears to show it's listening.

2

Say it out loud

Ramble. Restart a sentence. Think mid-thought. You don't have to speak in finished paragraphs — that's the point.

3

It's already written

Clean, punctuated text lands right where your cursor was. Read it once and hit send.

Not just transcription

It writes it the way
you'd have written it.

Ordinary dictation hands you a wall of everything you said, stumbles included, and leaves the tidying to you. SunoFlow hands you something you can actually send.

  • The ums, the false starts, and the sentences you began twice — gone
  • Capitals, commas and full stops put in for you
  • It never puts words in your mouth. It tidies what you said — it doesn't embroider it

Privacy

Your voice stays yours.

Dictation means letting something listen to you, so it's worth being straight about what happens next.

Your voice

Your recording is turned into words on your own Mac and then discarded. No audio is ever uploaded, and nothing you say is kept.

No account

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to subscribe to, and no profile of you anywhere. It's free, and there's no usage meter.

Your choice

The final polishing step sends the text — never the recording — to be tidied up. Don't want that? Switch it off and keep everything on your own machine.

Questions

Straight answers.

Which apps does it work in?
All of them. SunoFlow doesn't plug into apps one by one — it writes wherever your cursor already is. If you can type into it, you can talk into it: email, chat, documents, forms, comment boxes, search bars.
Does anyone hear my recordings?
No. Your voice is turned into text on your own Mac and the recording is thrown away immediately. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and no one — including us — can listen to it.
What about the text I dictate?
By default the finished text passes through a polishing step that tidies the grammar and punctuation, and that step runs online. It only ever sees the words, never the audio, and nothing is kept afterwards. If you'd rather nothing left your Mac at all, you can turn the polishing step off in Settings and still get your dictation.
Do I have to talk in a strange, robotic way?
No — that's rather the point. Speak the way you'd speak to a colleague. Pause, backtrack, start a sentence again. SunoFlow is built to clean all of that up, so you don't have to perform.
What does it cost?
Nothing. There's no subscription, no trial that runs out, and no limit on how much you dictate.
What do I need to run it?
A Mac with Apple silicon (any M-series machine) running macOS 13 or later. The first time you open it, SunoFlow will ask for permission to use your microphone and to type into other apps — it needs both to do its job.
Can I change the shortcut?
Yes. ⌥ Space is just the default — set it to whatever you like, along with which microphone it listens to.
Is there a Windows version?
A Windows version is in the works. The Mac app is the one that's ready today.
